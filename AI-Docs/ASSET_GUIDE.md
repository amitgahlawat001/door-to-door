## Production photo/video shopping list
Source from Pexels / Pixabay / Mixkit or replace with company-owned media:
- courier pickup
- warehouse / sorting
- delivery van on road
- city traffic / skyline
- courier at destination
- customer receiving parcel
- business fulfillment / packages

Always review the individual asset license before commercial use.

## Signature scroll sequence
Best long-term option:
1. Create a custom 8–12 second cinematic sequence.
2. Export H.264 MP4 or an optimized frame sequence.
3. Use a pinned GSAP ScrollTrigger timeline.

Alternative:
- 6–10 still images + SVG motion + crossfades for V1.
